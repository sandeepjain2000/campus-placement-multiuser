# Employer and new college signup

> **Section:** Accounts & registration  
> **Source:** help  
> **Audience:** all

Employer signup
Many deployments let employers register but keep login limited until a super admin (and later a college partnership) approves. Watch for activation email and TPO communication. Complete Company Profile early so colleges see a serious request.

New college signup
New institutes may also require platform activation. Until activated, students cannot usefully register against that campus.

While waiting
You may see pending states or login blocks. Contact platform support with the registered email rather than creating multiple accounts.

After activation
Employers still must win Campus Partnership approval per college. Colleges must configure Settings, enrollment key, and Placement Rules before the season opens.

Tip: one company should not register duplicate employer tenants for the same legal entity without ops guidance—it fragments partnerships and assessments.
