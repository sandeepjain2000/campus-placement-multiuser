# Drives, calendar, applications, interviews, offers

> **Section:** Students  
> **Source:** help  
> **Audience:** student

Browse Drives
Open from Home. Filter by mode (on-campus, virtual, hybrid, off-campus), status, and month/year. Read eligibility, deadline, and venue before applying. You can usually apply only when the drive is open (commonly approved or scheduled)—not while it is still requested or after cancelled/completed.

Placement calendar
Use the calendar view for drive-related dates so you do not miss clusters of visits in the same week.

My Applications
Track status labels such as applied, shortlisted, interview in progress, selected (formal offer pending), rejected, withdrawn, or on hold. You may withdraw while the product allows it; withdrawn applications generally cannot be updated by employers afterward.

Placement lock
After you accept a placement offer, the campus may lock you from new drive/job applies. That is intentional under Placement Rules—not a bug.

My Interviews
When slots are published, confirm time, mode, and panel instructions. Watch Alerts the same day.

My Offers
Pending offers can be accepted or declined. Colleges may limit how many offers you can accept and how many days you have after the first accept (acceptance window). Past-deadline offers may show as expired and reject further responses. Declining stores as rejected on the offer record.

Screenshot guidance: your live Browse Drives layout may differ slightly from help illustrations.
