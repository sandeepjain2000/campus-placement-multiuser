# Profile, My CVs, verification, and documents

> **Section:** Students  
> **Source:** help  
> **Audience:** student

My Profile
Keep department, roll number, batch year, CGPA, phone, and links accurate and consistent with institute records. Employers and TPOs shortlist using this data. Save after edits and refresh if a banner still shows stale values.

Verification
Your college may require profile verification before you can apply. A banner usually explains the pending state. Contact your TPO with your roll number if you are stuck longer than expected—do not create a second account.

My CVs
Upload one or more CVs (PDF as guided on screen). Your TPO can mark a CV verified. Some campuses require a verified CV before drive or internship apply succeeds. If apply fails with a CV message, upload/replace the file and ask the office to verify.

Documents
Upload certificates and ID proofs your institute requires. Replace outdated resumes in both Documents and My CVs when you revise them so recruiters do not download an old file.

Tips
• Use the email your college expects (often institute mail).
• After bulk data fixes by the TPO, sign out/in if fields look wrong.
• Alumni accounts may hide campus drives—use alumni menus instead.
