# Assessments, interviews, templates, clarifications

> **Section:** College admins (TPO)  
> **Source:** help  
> **Audience:** college, placement_committee

Hiring Assessment
Read-only mirror of employer hiring_result data (from their CSV or online update). Export for meetings; you cannot edit employer-submitted cells here—ask the employer to correct before they submit/lock if needed.

Interview Scheduling
Coordinate panels and rooms with employers; ensure students receive slots via the product (and Alerts).

Message templates
Create reusable college messages. Use Preview with sample data before sending. For system email templates inherited from the platform, restore an earlier retained system version when the UI offers version history—baseline remains for safety.

Bulk notifications
Preview audience and content, then send. Prefer templates for repeated announcements.

Clarifications
Collect student questions, batch them to employers, and publish official answers students can read.

Discussions
Moderate employer–student spaces; remove off-policy content.

Feature Ideas
Capture and vote on product ideas for your campus team so requests are not lost in email.

Tip: communication mistakes are hard to retract—preview every bulk send.
