# Dashboard, settings, and employers

> **Section:** College admins (TPO)  
> **Source:** help  
> **Audience:** college, placement_committee

Home and Dashboard
Start from the mega-menu Home for a map of all TPO tools, then use Dashboard/Overview widgets for pending partnerships, drives awaiting approval, and season pulse.

Settings
Configure placement season label, branding/logo, academic taxonomy, and policy knobs your deployment exposes: FCFS on/off, max offers per student, offer acceptance window days, CV-verification requirements, and related rules. Save deliberately—these affect student apply and offer accept immediately.

Employers directory
Browse companies tied to your campus. Open Partnership Requests to approve employers who should recruit or reject those who should not. Until approved, employers cannot effectively run campus-scoped hiring for you.

Tip: keep a short internal SOP for who may approve partnerships so committee members do not expect that button on their login.
