# Assessments, interviews, applications, offers, FCFS

> **Section:** Employers  
> **Source:** help  
> **Audience:** employer

Applications
Filter and review candidates who applied. Statuses move through applied → shortlisted → in_progress → selected / rejected / withdrawn / on_hold depending on your updates and hiring results.

Assessment map
Optional legacy screen for round display names. Modern hiring_result workflows (CSV / online) do not require the map.

Assessment uploads (CSV) and Assessment Update Online
Choose opportunity tab, campus, and target. Export fills campus students for the academic year; set hiring_result to Shortlist, Reject, Select, Decline, or Withdraw (blank = no decision). Upload or save online; fix staging errors before accept. Submit locks further edits on that path. See the detailed CSV article for the full sequence.

Hiring Results Dashboard
Read consolidated draft vs submitted results; export for internal review. College sees the same data read-only under Hiring Assessment.

Interviews
Schedule or confirm slots; students see them under My Interviews and Alerts.

FCFS — Shortlisted / Unavailable
When the campus enables FCFS, the first Select on a track (internship, placement/drives, or jobs) claims the student for other employers on that track. Open Shortlisted / Unavailable (FCFS) before forcing another Select.

Offers and Offer Templates
Create formal offers after selection (pending). Students accept/decline. Use Offer Templates for reusable wording. Bulk CSV is linked from the Offers page itself—not a sidebar item. You can revoke pending offers; reopening terminal statuses back to pending is available where the product allows.

Tip: Select is powerful—confirm FCFS impact before submitting hiring results.
