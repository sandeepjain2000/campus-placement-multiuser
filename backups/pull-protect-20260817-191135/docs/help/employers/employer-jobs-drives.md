# Jobs, drives, internships, and projects

> **Section:** Employers  
> **Source:** help  
> **Audience:** employer

Job postings
Create roles with compensation, skills, and eligibility (branches, min CGPA, batch year, backlogs as required). Campus posting limits may apply per college settings.

Placement drives
Create a drive against an approved campus. Required details typically include title, drive date, and drive type (on_campus, off_campus, virtual, hybrid). New drives start as requested. Students cannot apply until the college moves the drive to an open status (e.g. approved/scheduled). You may cancel while status is still open for cancel rules (requested/approved/scheduled/in_progress).

Internships and projects
Save draft to work privately, or publish with one or more approved campuses. Publish validation usually requires title, start/end (end on/after start), campuses, and eligibility fields. Each campus gets a visibility row pending until the TPO approves; students need published + approved visibility.

Internship outcomes
After hires start, use Internship Progress Reviews, Internship Supervisors, and Internship PPO (where licensed/enabled) to capture mid-term feedback, guides, and pre-placement offer steps. Coordinate with the college Internship Results views.

Tip: if students never appear, confirm partnership approved → Active campus correct → college approved the drive/listing.
