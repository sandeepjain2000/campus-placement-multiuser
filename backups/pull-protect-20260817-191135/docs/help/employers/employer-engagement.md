# Sponsorships, marketplace, templates, discussions

> **Section:** Employers  
> **Source:** help  
> **Audience:** employer

Sponsorships and Startup Seed Funding
Publish or manage sponsorship / seed-funding opportunities toward campuses. Follow payment or confirmation steps shown on those screens. Colleges may acknowledge or thank sponsors via system email templates.

Campus guest needs
Respond to college requests for lectures, workshops, or guest sessions. Confirm dates against your calendar to avoid drive clashes.

Marketplace
Browse the platform service catalog and place orders when Marketplace is enabled for employers. Track order status in the Marketplace menu.

Communication templates
Use employer email/communication templates for recurring outreach where configured. Prefer templates over ad-hoc copy for auditability.

Clarifications
When the college publishes a clarification batch for your company, answer officially there—students read the published answers, not side emails alone.

Discussions
Participate in campus threads when enabled. Keep responses aligned with what you will honor in interviews/offers.

Mentorship
Volunteer on Student Mentorship Requests when the college posts needs.

Tip: engagement modules are optional per deployment—if a menu is missing, it may be disabled for your tenant.
