# What is PlacementHub?

> **Section:** Platform basics  
> **Source:** help  
> **Audience:** all

PlacementHub is a multi-tenant campus placement and engagement platform. It gives students, college placement offices (TPOs), placement committees, employers, and platform operators one shared system for the full season—not scattered spreadsheets and email threads.

What it covers
• Recruiting: job postings, placement drives, internships, projects, and alumni job flows where enabled.
• Pipeline: applications, hiring results (CSV or online), interviews, offers, and FCFS (first-confirm) locks.
• Campus ops: enrollment keys, student/CV verification, employer partnerships, calendars, clarifications, message templates, placement rules, and reports.
• Engagement: sponsorships, guest needs, marketplace services, mentorship, and feature ideas (by role).
• Platform ops: onboarding colleges/employers, users, system email templates, email delivery logs, and global settings (including test-environment email redirect).

Why it matters
Everyone sees the same drive status, application outcome, and offer state. Colleges enforce eligibility and fairness rules once; employers run selection against an approved campus; students get a single place for applies, interviews, and offer decisions.

Your experience depends on your signed-in role. The Home screen is a full-screen mega-menu of destinations for that role. Use Help (sparkle) or this documentation if a menu name is unfamiliar.
