# How the roles connect (one season story)

> **Section:** Use case flows  
> **Source:** help  
> **Audience:** all

One simplified season story (happy path)

1. Super admin enables the college and employer on the platform (and activates registrations if required).
2. College admin shares the enrollment key with students and approves the employer’s Campus Partnership.
3. Employer completes profile, selects the Active campus, and creates a Placement Drive (status requested) or publishes an Internship (visibility pending).
4. College admin approves the drive or internship visibility (watching calendar clashes for drives).
5. Students with verified profile/CV apply while the opportunity is open and they meet eligibility.
6. Employer runs assessments (CSV or online), may Select candidates (FCFS may lock tracks), and schedules interviews; college monitors Hiring Assessment read-only.
7. Employer or college records a formal Offer (pending). Student accepts or declines under college offer rules; accepting a placement offer may set placement lock.
8. Clarifications, discussions, calendars, and reports keep the office and leadership informed. Placement committee may observe Students/Applications without changing approvals.

Deployments may skip steps (auto-approved employers) or add steps (extra assessment rounds). When in doubt, follow the role-specific flow articles above and Placement Rules on your campus.
