# Employer — hire on a campus

> **Section:** Use case flows  
> **Source:** help  
> **Audience:** all

Goal: unlock an approved campus, publish roles, run a fair selection pipeline, and close formal offers.

Step-by-step
1. Complete Company Profile (logo, description, contacts) so TPOs and students recognize your brand.
2. Open Campus Partnerships and request tie-ups. Wait until status is approved. Pending/rejected campuses cannot host campus-scoped drives the same way.
3. On Home, confirm Active campus (change campus if you hire at multiple institutes). Recruiting lists and exports follow this partnership.
4. Create Job Postings and/or Placement Drives for that campus. A new drive starts as requested until the college approves it. For internships, publish with campuses selected; visibility stays pending until the TPO approves.
5. As applications arrive, use Applications plus Assessment uploads (CSV) or Assessment Update Online to set hiring_result (Shortlist, Reject, Select, Decline, Withdraw). Review Hiring Results Dashboard. Schedule interviews as needed.
6. Remember FCFS: the first Select on a track can make the student Unavailable to other employers on that track. Check Shortlisted / Unavailable (FCFS).
7. After selection, create formal Offers (or use Offer Templates / Offers-page CSV). Students accept or decline on My Offers. Respond to Clarifications and Discussions when the college publishes them.

Tip: empty pipelines almost always mean partnership not approved, wrong Active campus, or drive/internship not yet college-approved.
