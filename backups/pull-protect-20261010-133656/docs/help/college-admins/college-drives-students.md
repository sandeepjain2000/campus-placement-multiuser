# Drives, students, CVs, enrollment, applications, offers

> **Section:** College admins (TPO)  
> **Source:** help  
> **Audience:** college, placement_committee

Placement Drives
Unapproved tab: employer drives in requested. Review date, mode, and venue. Approve to open for students (product may return calendar clash 409—confirm force only if you intentionally overlap). Reject/cancel paths remove the drive from open student apply. Active tabs group approved/scheduled/in_progress; rejected/cancelled appear under rejected-style filters.

Internships
Approve or reject each campus visibility row. Students need employer published + your approved visibility. You can often re-approve from rejected when a listing is corrected.

Students
Search and open student detail to verify profiles, fix data, and verify CVs when your rules require verified CVs before apply. Unverified students generate “cannot apply” tickets—clear the queue before big drive weeks.

Enrollment key
Copy/rotate from the Enrollment key screen. Treat like a password. After rotation, tell students the new key; old keys fail registration.

Applications and Offers
Monitor pipeline health. Create or upload formal offers when your office records offers (Offers page CSV link). Enforce max offers and windows via Placement Rules rather than manual tracking alone.
