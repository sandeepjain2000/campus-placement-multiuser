# Placement committee (read-only limits)

> **Section:** College admins (TPO)  
> **Source:** help  
> **Audience:** college, placement_committee

Who this is for
Faculty or student committee members who need visibility into the season without full TPO powers.

What you usually can do
• Open college Home (reduced mega-menu).
• Browse Overview, Students, and Applications for situational awareness.
• Use shared Alerts and Feedback.
• Coordinate offline with the TPO using what you see in the lists.

What you usually cannot do
• Approve or reject employer partnerships.
• Approve or reject drives or internship visibility.
• Edit Placement Rules, Settings, or enrollment keys.
• Create, revoke, or bulk-upload offers.
• Publish clarifications or manage message templates as admin (unless your deployment explicitly expands access).

If a button is missing
That is expected. Ask your college admin to perform the action. After the TPO changes your role, sign out and sign in again.

Tip: do not share TPO credentials to “get around” read-only—use the proper college_admin account instead.
