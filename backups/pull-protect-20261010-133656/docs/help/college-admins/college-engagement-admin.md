# Calendar, marketplace, rules, reports, internship outcomes

> **Section:** College admins (TPO)  
> **Source:** help  
> **Audience:** college, placement_committee

Calendar and Events
Publish placement calendar entries; import/export ICS where available. Drive approve may warn when a drive date clashes with calendar items.

Guest faculty and lectures
Track guest engagements so rooms and marketing stay aligned with drive weeks.

Marketplace
Order services from the platform catalog when enabled for colleges.

Placement Rules
Document and set CGPA floors, backlog limits, max offers per student, acceptance windows, and FCFS behaviour for internship / placement / jobs tracks. These rules power apply gates and offer accept errors students see.

Internship outcomes
Use Internship Progress Reviews, Guides, PPO, and Internship Results to monitor post-apply internship quality—not only the initial select.

Infrastructure
Record rooms/labs/logistics your office manages.

Reports and Audit reports
Export season metrics and compliance views for leadership. Prefer product reports over manual spreadsheets when data already lives in PlacementHub.

Tip: change FCFS mid-season only with a written office decision—employers rely on Unavailable lists.
