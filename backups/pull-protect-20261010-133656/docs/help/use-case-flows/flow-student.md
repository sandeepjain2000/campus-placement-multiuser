# Student — typical placement journey

> **Section:** Use case flows  
> **Source:** help  
> **Audience:** all

Goal: get verified, discover open opportunities, apply on time, attend interviews, and decide on offers within your college’s rules.

Step-by-step
1. Register (or sign in) with your institute email and the campus enrollment key from the placement office—not your roll number as the key.
2. Complete My Profile (academics, contacts, links). Upload Documents and at least one CV under My CVs. Wait if your college must verify your profile or CV before apply is allowed.
3. Open Home → Browse Drives (and Internships / Projects as relevant). Filter by mode, status, and date. Check the Placement calendar for visit and deadline dates.
4. Apply only when the drive/program is open and you meet eligibility (CGPA, branch/batch, resume/CV). Confirm success under My Applications.
5. Watch Alerts for interview invites. Open My Interviews when schedules appear; note venue/mode and any prep instructions from clarifications.
6. Read Clarifications for official company Q&A. Use Discussions only if your college enables them. Use Mentor Connect if mentorship is offered.
7. When a formal offer appears under My Offers, accept or decline within college limits (max simultaneous accepts and acceptance window). Declining records as rejected on the offer.

What “blocked” usually means
Profile or CV not verified; CGPA/branch mismatch; drive still requested/cancelled; deadline passed; already applied; FCFS internship lock; or placement lock after accepting a placement offer.

Tip: fix eligibility and verification first—support cannot override college rules from Help chat alone.
