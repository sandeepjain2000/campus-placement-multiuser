# Company profile and campus partnerships

> **Section:** Employers  
> **Source:** help  
> **Audience:** employer

Company Profile
Upload a clear logo, write a concise company description, and keep recruiter contacts current. Colleges and students judge seriousness from this page. Incomplete profiles slow partnership approvals.

Campus Partnerships
Request a tie-up with each institute you want to hire from. Statuses typically include pending, approved, rejected (and sometimes revoked/blacklisted in UI). Until approved, campus-scoped create/list actions are limited or empty.

Active campus
On Home you should see which campus is active. Use Change campus / Campus Partnerships when you work across multiple approved colleges. Exports, drives, and assessment targets follow the active partnership—wrong campus is the most common “missing students” cause.

Events calendar
Align visit days and shared milestones with the college calendar to reduce clashes (colleges may block approve on calendar conflicts).

Screenshot: partnership tables show request actions and status chips; your columns may vary slightly.
