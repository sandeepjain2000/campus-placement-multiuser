# Navigation, home, and sidebar

> **Section:** Platform basics  
> **Source:** help  
> **Audience:** all

Home (mega-menu hub)
After sign-in you land on your role Home. Destinations are grouped in columns (for example Core / Overview, Student Opportunities, Candidate Pipeline). Quick-action chips jump to frequent screens (drives, applications, campus change, alerts). Use the search box on Home to filter destinations by name.

Returning to Home
On inner pages, choose Home in the top bar (or the Home link in the sidebar) to reopen the full map. Employers may also see an Active campus banner on Home with a link to change campus.

Sidebar on inner pages
After you open a category, the sidebar usually shows only that category’s links so the list stays short. Expand or switch categories as needed. On phones and small tablets, open the menu from the top bar.

Session chrome
The top bar typically shows your name/role, notifications (bell), theme toggle, and Sign out. A session timer may appear in some deployments.

Tip: bookmarking a deep link is fine, but if you get redirected to login or another role’s home, your session expired or you are signed in as a different role.
