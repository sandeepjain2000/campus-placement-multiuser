# Internships, projects, outcomes, clarifications, discussions

> **Section:** Students  
> **Source:** help  
> **Audience:** student

Internships and projects
You only see listings that are published by the employer and approved by your college for your campus (plus an approved employer partnership). Draft or pending-visibility items stay hidden.

Applying and FCFS
Apply from the internship/program screens. If you are Selected on an internship while FCFS is enabled, you typically cannot apply to other internships—the product enforces a single selected internship. Check Pending / Not processed internship menus if your campus uses them.

Internship outcomes
Where enabled, use student menu items for Internship Progress Reviews, PPO responses, or related feedback. Follow deadlines from your TPO and employer supervisor.

Clarifications
Read official Q&A batches published by the placement office for a company/drive. This is not private chat with HR—answers are campus-visible as published.

Discussions
Join moderated threads only when your college turns them on. Keep posts professional; TPOs can moderate.

Mentor Connect
Request mentorship where the college posts needs and employers volunteer. Track request status in Mentorship Requests.

Tip: if an internship disappears after you saw it, the college may have rejected visibility or the employer unpublished—refresh and check with the TPO.
