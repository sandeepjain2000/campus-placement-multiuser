# Alerts, exports, and tips

> **Section:** Students  
> **Source:** help  
> **Audience:** student

Alerts
Treat the bell as mandatory during drive weeks: interview changes and offer notices often land here first.

My data export
Use when you need a personal archive of your data. Follow on-screen steps; keep the download secure.

Practical tips
• Keep phone and department aligned with institute records to avoid CSV/shortlist mismatches.
• Apply early—do not wait for the last hour of a deadline in case of verification issues.
• If you are alumni, do not expect campus drive menus; use alumni job flows.
• For account lockouts or wrong role, contact your TPO or platform support with your registered email.

Still stuck: use Feedback with role, URL, and time—or ask Help with the exact error text from the apply banner.
